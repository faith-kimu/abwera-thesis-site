Predicting Re-Engagement in HIV Care After Missed Appointments

Faith Upile Kimu · MSc thesis

This thesis studies how routinely collected clinic records can help identify people living with HIV who miss a scheduled appointment, and how machine learning models might flag those at risk of not returning to care.

This page is an overview only. It contains no patient data. The analysis and dashboard are kept private.
